## USPSTF Recommendation Summary
The United States Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF) provides evidence-based recommendations on clinical preventive services. Below are the definitive screening recommendations for Oral Cancer: Screening:

- **Population**: Asymptomatic Adults
  - **Recommendation**: The USPSTF concludes that the current evidence is insufficient to assess the balance of benefits and harms of screening for oral cancer in asymptomatic adults.

Go to the Clinical Considerations section for suggestions for practice regarding the I statement.

Literature scans conducted in July 2023 in the MEDLINE database and the Cochrane Library showed a lack of new evidence to support an updated systematic review on the topic at this time. See the Literature Surveillance Report under the Supporting Evidence section of this webpage.
  - **Grade**: **I**

## Clinical Considerations & Implementation
Clinicians should review individual patient risk factors, screening intervals, and shared decision-making considerations.

## Rationale and Balance of Benefits and Harms
Screening effectiveness depends on disease prevalence, test accuracy, interval adherence, and the balance between early detection mortality benefits and potential screening harms (false positives, unnecessary biopsies, overdiagnosis).
